## Camping Kit 1.8

**New: Sit button**
- Near a campfire, a small button on the tent's rim sits you down by the fire, so you pick up the camp's bonuses without typing /sit. It shows only while you have Campfire Nearby, and has its own key binding.

**New: Fade when left open**
- A new setting fades the open kit after the mouse has been off it for a while (5 seconds to start, adjustable from 1 to 30). It stays open and comes back when you mouse over it. It doesn't fade while a drawer is out.
- New "Faded opacity" slider sets how far the kit fades, for this and for Fade when idle.
- Fading now eases in and out instead of snapping.
- The timers under the tent and the camp's row fade along with the slots.

**Changes**
- Settings are grouped into sections: General, Look, Fading, In combat, then Camping Kit's own (profession buttons, timers, camps, map, reminders).
- Slots and profession buttons are always round. The square option is gone.
